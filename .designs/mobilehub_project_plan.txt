# Project Name: MobileHub
## Design Goals
- **Persona**: Tech-savvy customers and efficient platform admins.
- **Style**: Modern, clean, minimal, premium.
- **Palette**: White background, Deep Blue (#1E3A8A) and Dark Gray (#1F2937) accents.
- **Components**: Material Design inspired, high-fidelity product cards, data-rich dashboards.

## Screen List Proposal

### Phase 1: Customer Journey (Mobile-First)
1. **Landing/Home Page**: Hero banner, trending phones, deals, and brand sections.
2. **Product Listing Page**: Grid view with advanced filters (RAM, Camera, 5G) and AI recommendations.
3. **Product Details Page**: Multi-image gallery, specs table, and YouTube review integration.
4. **Shopping Cart & Checkout**: Streamlined multi-step process from cart to order confirmation.
5. **User Dashboard & Order Tracking**: Profile management and visual order progress timeline.

### Phase 2: Authentication
6. **Login / Registration**: Clean, secure entry points for users.

### Phase 3: Admin Experience (Desktop-Optimized)
7. **Admin Dashboard**: Analytics overview with revenue, orders, and sales graphs.
8. **Product Management**: Interface for adding/editing inventory and specifications.
9. **Order & Customer Management**: Tools for tracking fulfillment and managing user data.

## Features to Highlight
- **AI Recommendation Engine**: "Best Gaming," "Best Camera," and budget-friendly suggestions.
- **YouTube Integration**: Expert video reviews embedded on product pages.
- **Visual Order Tracking**: Interactive timeline for status updates.
